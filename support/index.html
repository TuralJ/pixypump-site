Getting started

What do I need to use PixyPump?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. PixyPump is an iPhone app. There is no Android version.

Do I need an account?

No. PixyPump works fully without one, and everything you log stays on your iPhone. An account is optional. You can create one with Apple, Google or your email address to keep your progress safe.

Is PixyPump free?

Tracking workouts, food and progress is free. PixyPump Pro is an optional upgrade with extras. See the Pro section below.

Workouts

How do I change my plan?

Open the Workout tab and tap the pencil. You can rename days, swap exercises, change the number of sets, and add or remove days. You can also switch to a different ready-made plan, and the app asks you to confirm first.

Can I change just today's workout?

Yes. Skipping, swapping or reordering exercises during a workout only changes that day. Your plan stays as it was.

How does the rest timer work?

There is one rest timer for the whole app. It keeps running while you move between exercises. When it ends, your iPhone alerts you. In Settings, in the Training section, you can turn the automatic timer after each set on or off and choose your usual rest length.

I logged a set by mistake. How do I fix it?

Tap the set to edit it. When you delete a set, a meal or a weigh-in, an Undo button appears for a few seconds.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes. Choose kg or lb in Settings, in the Profile section. Your history is stored precisely, so switching never changes a number you logged.

Food and calories

How do I log food?

Open the Calories tab and pick a meal. You can scan a barcode, search your saved foods, create your own food, or use quick add to type in calories.

A scanned product is wrong or not found

Barcode details come from Open Food Facts, a free community database. Some products are missing or have mistakes, so check the label, especially if you have allergies. If a product is missing, create your own food or use quick add.

How do I set my calorie and macro goals?

Go to Settings, then the Food section, and tap your daily goals. You can type your own numbers or use the calculator, which suggests goals from your age, height, weight and aim. These are general estimates and not medical advice.

Your account

How do I create an account or sign in?

Open Settings and go to the Account section, then tap Create account or sign in. You can continue with Apple, Google or email. With email, we send you a 6-digit code and there is no password to remember.

How do I sign out?

In Settings, go to the Account section and tap Sign out.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open Settings and go to the Account section.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Choose to delete the account only, or the account and the data on this iPhone.

Your account and anything saved with it are removed. This cannot be undone. If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Create a backup on your old phone: in Settings, go to Data & privacy and tap Create backup. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install PixyPump, go to the same place and tap Restore from backup.

PixyPump Pro and subscriptions

What does Pro include?

Pro unlocks every look and turns on a weekly streak freeze. Tracking stays free forever. Pro adds extras on top.

What plans are there?

Monthly, yearly and lifetime, which you pay for once. Prices are shown in the app by the App Store in your own currency. A free trial is offered on plans where you are eligible, and the price after the trial is shown before you confirm.

I paid, but Pro is not unlocked
  1. Open Settings and tap the PixyPump Pro row to open the Pro screen.
  2. Tap Restore purchases.
  3. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

If that does not work, email us with your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, and we cannot cancel them for you. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and choose PixyPump. You can also tap Manage subscription on the Pro screen in the app.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Your workouts, food, body weight, plan and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. Deleting the app removes them, so make a backup first if you want to keep them.

How do I back up or export my data?

In Settings, go to Data & privacy. Create backup saves one file with everything, which you can restore later. Export as spreadsheet (CSV) gives you your workouts and food as files you can open in Numbers or Excel.

How do I delete all my data?

In Settings, go to Data & privacy and tap Delete my data. It erases everything PixyPump stored on this iPhone, and you can choose to remove the workouts PixyPump saved to Apple Health too. This cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you might want your history back.

What leaves my iPhone?

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. If you create an account, your sign-in details are kept so you can sign in. PixyPump has no ads and does not track you. The full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are in the app, in Settings under Data & privacy.

How does Apple Health work?

It is optional. If you turn it on, PixyPump can read your body weight and save your workouts to Apple Health. You choose exactly what is allowed in the Health app and can change it at any time.

Troubleshooting

The rest timer did not alert me

Check that rest alerts are allowed. In PixyPump, open Settings and look at the rest alert line in the Training section. If it says alerts are off, tap Open iPhone Settings and allow notifications for PixyPump. If it offers Allow rest alerts, tap that. Also check that a Focus mode is not silencing the app, and that iOS is up to date.

The barcode scanner does not work

PixyPump needs camera access to scan. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap PixyPump and turn on Camera. Hold the phone steady in good light. If a product still does not scan, use quick add or create your own food.

My history is gone after reinstalling

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. If you made a backup, install PixyPump again and restore it from Settings, in Data & privacy. If you did not, we cannot recover it, because your data is stored on your phone and not on our servers.

Pro prices do not load

Check your internet connection and that you are signed in to the App Store. Close and reopen the Pro screen. If the prices still do not appear, email us.
