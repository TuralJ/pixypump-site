Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

PixyPump is built so your training data stays with you. An account is optional. Without one, everything you log stays on your iPhone. If you create an account, we keep your email address and name so you can sign in. There are no ads, and PixyPump does not track you or sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your workouts and sets, training plan, food and calories, body weight and measurements, goals, and the profile details you choose to enter, such as name, sex, birth date and height. It also holds your settings, your buddy's look and your game progress. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by iOS.

Your account

Creating an account is optional. You can sign in with Apple, Google or your email address. If you do, we keep your email address and name, and the sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication service, which processes that information for us so you can sign in. We use it for signing you in and nothing else. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Settings, in the Account section. Deleting your account removes it and anything saved with it.

PixyPump Pro and payments

PixyPump Pro is an optional purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple tells the app whether you have Pro, so the app can unlock it. Cancelling, refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple.

What leaves your iPhone

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to look up the product. Like any website, they can see your device's IP address. Their own privacy policy applies to that lookup. Account sign-in, if you use it, is described above.

If you open an exercise video, YouTube opens in your browser or the YouTube app, under Google's privacy policy. Nothing else about you or your training is sent anywhere.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, PixyPump can read your body weight from Apple Health and save your workouts to it. You choose exactly what is allowed in the Health app, and you can change it at any time. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with anyone by PixyPump.

Backups and exports

Backups and spreadsheet exports are files you create yourself. They contain your data, so keep them somewhere safe. Where they go, such as Files, iCloud Drive or email, is up to you.

Notifications, camera and alarms

Notifications and alarms are used for your rest timer and your streak reminders. The camera is used only to scan barcodes. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

In Settings, under Data & privacy, choose Delete my data to erase everything PixyPump has stored on this iPhone. You can also choose to remove the workouts PixyPump saved to Apple Health. To delete your account, go to Settings, then the Account section, and choose Delete account. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone. If you cannot do this in the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account.

Your rights

You can view, export, correct or delete your data in the app at any time. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection. To use them, or to ask anything about your account data, email us. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

PixyPump is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
